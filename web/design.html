Design

Design a disordered sequence
Paste a disordered region. This generates new sequences whose predicted conformational ensemble matches it, while letting you choose how much of the original amino-acid composition to keep.

Ensembles are predicted with STARLING; sequences are optimized with GOOSE. Every run starts from a GS repeat, so nothing is carried over from the input except what you pin below.
